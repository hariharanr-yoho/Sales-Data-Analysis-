Sales Data Analysis – Final Report
1. Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyse sales performance across different product categories and identify sales trends, profitability, and business opportunities.

2. Dataset Description

The dataset contains 30 simulated sales records from January to June 2026. It includes five columns: Date, Category, Sales, Profit, and Quantity.

Tools used: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

3. Three Key Insights
Electronics generated the highest revenue: Electronics recorded total sales of 237,000 currency units, followed by Furniture with 137,000 and Clothing with 95,000.
Clothing had the highest quantity sold: Clothing recorded 188 units sold, while Electronics recorded 113 and Furniture recorded 85.
Sales increased over time: Monthly sales increased from 49,000 in January to 102,000 in May, followed by a small decline to 99,000 in June.
4. Two Recommendations
Maintain sufficient Electronics inventory because it generates the highest revenue and profit.
Improve Clothing pricing, promotions, and product selection to increase revenue while maintaining strong sales volume.
5. Limitations
The dataset contains only 30 records.
The dataset is simulated and does not represent actual business transactions.
The analysis covers only six months.
Customer demographics and operating expenses are not available.
6. Conclusion

The analysis identifies Electronics as the highest revenue-generating category and Clothing as the category with the highest quantity sold. Businesses can use these findings to improve inventory management and marketing strategies.
